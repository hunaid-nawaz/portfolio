# Hunaid Portfolio

Public static site for Hunaid Nawaz: the subject of this Site, his CaseStudies, Roles, and Posts.

## Language

**Person**:
Hunaid Nawaz, the subject of the Site.
_Avoid_: user, author, profile

**CaseStudy**:
A shipped product or system shown under Work.
_Avoid_: project, work item, role

**Role**:
A job held, shown only on About.
_Avoid_: putting a Role on `/work`

**Lane**:
A layer of the Person’s practice (backend, frontend, cloud, payments and integrations).
_Avoid_: skill category, spectrum

**Toolbox**:
Tools grouped by how they are used.
_Avoid_: skill bars, percentages

**Post**:
A writing piece under `/case-studies`.
_Avoid_: blog, article (in code and filenames)

**Recruiter**:
A visitor evaluating the Person for a full-time role.
_Avoid_: mixing this CTA with Client copy

**Client**:
A visitor evaluating the Person for contract or product work.
_Avoid_: customer, buyer
