Insurance brokers needed a path from intake to a quote that could go out the door. The product was AppEase: structured quote workflows, stakeholder-defined steps, and infrastructure that stayed up while a deal was in motion.

I built those workflows in Rails and ran the app on AWS for availability. The work was delivery with people who used the tool, not a greenfield rewrite for its own sake.

What had to be true: a broker could complete a quote without improvising the process in a spreadsheet, and the next change didn’t require tribal knowledge of an undocumented flow.
